    THE BIGGER PICTURE / THREE IDEAS

    It's not just the clothes. It's the way they're made, released and experienced.

    01 / CREATIVE OWNERSHIP

    The partner changes the brief.

    Atlanta Dream brought in Monarch Commerce to reshape the store experience, while Athleta will become an off-court apparel partner for Golden State. One changes the retail operation; the other develops a distinct apparel proposition.

    Planning question: Where would a specialist add something a traditional supplier cannot?

    Dream announcement ↗ Athleta announcement ↗
    02 / RELEASE STRATEGY

    From one drop to a year of reasons to return.

    For its 30th season, the WNBA announced a Legacy Trail series unveiling a new licensee collaboration on the 30th of each month. That is a different rhythm from a single launch, although it says nothing about sales.

    Planning question: What deserves a one-off launch, and what could become a recurring product story?

    WNBA official announcement ↗
    03 / CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

    The retail moment is part of the product.

    At WNBA Live in 2026, Playa Society hosted a branded retail experience and Pro Standard offered customization and player appearances. The collection is one part of the experience; how fans encounter it is another.

    Planning question: Which products deserve an experience instead of only a listing?
